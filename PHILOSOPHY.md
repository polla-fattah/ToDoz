# The ToDoz Philosophy
## How ToDoz Embodies Sovereign Personal Software

> *Your data belongs to you. Your tools should respect your time, preserve your freedom, and remain useful without depending on someone else's infrastructure.*

ToDoz was created to embody the philosophy of [Sovereign Personal Software](https://sovereign.polla.dev/). The full manifesto, with its six principles, seven engineering tests, an interactive [evaluator](https://sovereign.polla.dev/evaluator/), and a [reading edition](https://sovereign.polla.dev/read/), lives on its own site. This page covers only how ToDoz applies it.

## How ToDoz Applies the Six Principles

1. **Sovereignty:** Your tasks live in a local database you own. ToDoz uses documented formats and self-hosted sync, and never requires an account with a third party.
2. **Locality:** The local database is the primary working environment, so everything works offline. Sync runs separately, with explicit status and predictable conflict handling.
3. **Predictability:** Recurring schedules, reminders, and filters follow deterministic, testable rules. Invalid or ambiguous input is reported, never silently guessed.
4. **Responsiveness:** ToDoz targets a visible response within 100 milliseconds for routine actions, and avoids unnecessary notifications and interface complexity.
5. **Focused Interoperability:** ToDoz handles task execution only. It connects to Markdown repositories, journals, and calendars through documented interfaces and open formats.
6. **Longevity:** Your data is kept in documented, widely supported formats, with portable exports (CSV, ICS, and JSON) and verified backups.

## AI in the Build, Not in the Product

ToDoz was built with AI coding agents, which lowered the cost of development. The application contains no AI features today, and every essential operation runs on deterministic rules.

**AI helped build ToDoz. It does not have to become ToDoz.**

## What ToDoz Refuses to Become

- **Mandatory accounts:** No cloud account or subscription is needed for essential functions.
- **AI in predictable operations:** No AI inference where deterministic rules belong.
- **Telemetry:** No tracking or undisclosed collection of personal activity.
- **Feature bloat:** No duplication of tools that do their job better.
- **Hidden sync behavior and lock-in:** Synchronization stays visible, and your data stays portable.

Because AI makes features cheap to build, the discipline of refusing them matters more, not less.
