The ToDoz Philosophy

How ToDoz embodies Sovereign Personal Software
“Software should serve people without claiming permanent custody over their information, choices, or work.”

ToDoz was built to test and embody the philosophy of Sovereign Personal Software in a daily productivity tool. The full manifesto—together with its ten intellectual lineage dossiers, its evidence-led evaluation methodology, the Sovereign Index, and cloud-exit runbooks—lives on its own site. This page explains how ToDoz applies the framework in practice.

Foundational Architecture

How ToDoz Applies the Four Principles

In the Sovereign Software framework, four foundational principles stand in a deliberate conceptual relationship: Sovereignty defines the goal; Longevity protects it across time; Locality provides a primary means of independence; and Transparency and Control make authority meaningful in operation.

Principle I · Sovereignty Authority belongs to the user

Practical Custody and Exit

Your tasks, projects, hierarchy, comments, and completion history live in a local SQLite database you control. ToDoz requires no commercial cloud account or subscription. Its optional synchronization server (server/, Dart + SQLite) runs on your own infrastructure, authenticating devices with single-use pairing codes and revocable per-device tokens.

→ You can export, migrate, or unpair from a sync server at any moment without losing existing work.
Principle II · Longevity Information outlives software

Data, Functional, and Knowledge Longevity

Your records are stored in documented Drift/SQLite schemas and exported in open, human-inspectable formats: full-fidelity JSON backups, CSV project tables, ICS calendar feeds, and plain-text lists. Automatic local folder backups and backwards-compatible restoration (including TaskFlow 1.0 backup imports) ensure your records remain recoverable independently of any binary.

→ Even if you stop running ToDoz decades from now, your task archive remains readable with standard tools.
Principle III · Locality Independence is designed in

Offline by Design, Sync by Choice

On Windows and Android, the local SQLite database is the primary working environment. Creating, completing, rescheduling, searching, and running complex Boolean filter queries require zero network connectivity. Network calls occur only when you pair a self-hosted sync server or optional Google Calendar integration, and offline edits queue locally until your server is reachable.

→ A failed network or unreachable server never erases your ability to work.
Principle IV · Transparency and Control Consequential behavior remains governable

Deterministic Rules and Inspectable State

Recurring schedules (every! 2 months, every last workday), reminders, and Boolean filters run on deterministic, unit-tested rules in packages/taskflow_core. Ambiguous grammar is explicitly refused rather than silently guessed; every change is recorded in an inspectable activity log with Undo; and ToDoz states transparently that self-hosted sync merges field-by-field (LWW) without end-to-end encryption.

→ The software never quietly guesses your schedule or hides how synchronization resolves conflicts.
Verification & Craftsmanship

Supporting Engineering Qualities (T6 & T7)

The Sovereign Software evaluation methodology distinguishes core eligibility requirements (E1–E5) and verification procedures (T1–T5) from supporting software qualities (T6 Interoperability & Maintainability and T7 Human Experience & Performance). ToDoz treats both supporting qualities as essential engineering craftsmanship:

Procedure T6 · Interoperability & Maintainability Supporting Means

Bounded Scope, Open Interfaces

ToDoz handles task execution and scheduling without trying to swallow your long-form notes or reference library. It connects cleanly to Markdown vaults, journals, and external systems through a documented REST API with personal tokens, signed incoming and outgoing webhooks, ICS calendar feeds, and CSV/JSON import and export.

Procedure T7 · Human Experience & Performance Supporting Quality

Immediate Local Feedback

Because routine actions read and write directly to local SQLite storage without waiting for cloud round-trips, ToDoz delivers immediate sub-100ms feedback on local interactions, keyboard-driven Quick Add, and OS-native scheduled notifications without dark patterns or clutter.

Artificial Intelligence

AI in the Build, Not in the Product

AI coding agents lowered the development cost of building ToDoz across Flutter, React, and Dart. Inside the product, however, every essential operation—recurrence calculation, filter evaluation, reminder scheduling, and field-level sync—runs on deterministic, testable code with zero dependence on external AI models or API keys.

AI helped make ToDoz practical to build. Your authority over your tasks never depends on an AI provider.
Boundaries

What ToDoz Refuses to Become

✕
Captive data or mandatory accounts: No commercial cloud account or subscription is needed to use, back up, or export your work.
✕
Opaque automation in predictable operations: No probabilistic AI guessing where deterministic scheduling and filter grammar belong.
✕
Silent loss through synchronization: Field-by-field merge rules are documented, device access is revocable, and sync is never treated as a substitute for independent JSON backups.
✕
Concealed dependencies and surveillance: Zero tracking telemetry, zero analytics SDKs, and zero unannounced background calls.
✕
Unbounded feature bloat: Because AI makes features cheap to generate, the discipline of keeping the architecture focused and maintainable matters more, not less.
