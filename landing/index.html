ENGINEERED TO OUTPERFORM

Built with Craftsmanship, Not Bloat

Every line of ToDoz was written with purpose. No tracking cookies, no sluggish web-wrapped Electron shells, and zero AI hallucinations.

100% Offline-First (Drift SQLite)

Your tasks reside on your device first. Complete tasks, reorder boards, and filter views with instant sub-100ms response times without internet access.

Mathematical Recurrence Engine

Deterministic natural-language parser. Supports every! 2 months (completion-based), every 1st wed, every last workday, and explicit refusal of invalid syntax.

Google Calendar Time-Grid

Full 15-minute slot time-grid in Day, 3-Day, Week, and Month layouts. Drag cards to reschedule, drag borders to adjust duration, or switch to Kanban Boards and Lists.

Todoist-Style Power Filters

Full Boolean query language: (today | overdue) & #Research, p1 & overdue, p4 & today, and due before: next monday. Save custom views with live error checking.

Zero-Password Device Pairing

No passwords stored on servers. Join devices via short-lived cryptographic pairing codes. Revoke stolen devices instantly.

Self-Hosted Sync Server

Runs in a single Docker container. Uses field-level Last-Write-Wins (LWW) resolution, Tailscale private mode, and zero third-party cloud lock-in.

FLEXIBLE TOPOLOGY

One Task Model. Three View Dimensions.

Switch between chronological planning, visual kanban pipelines, and deep hierarchy without losing a single tag.

Google Calendar-Style Precision

Drawn natively in Flutter and React. Features a "now" line, overlapping side-by-side tasks, duration adjustment handles in 15-minute increments, and auto-scroll when dragging near viewport edges.

// Calendar Views Supported natively: • Day View - Micro-schedule today's hours with exact 15m time blocks • 3-Day View - Multi-day sprint planning for high-intensity work • Week View - Broad 7-day tactical horizon across all projects • Month View - High-level bird's eye view with drag-and-drop reschedule

Deterministic Natural Language Rules

Built with an explicit parser in pure Dart. No LLMs guessing what you meant. Every rule executes with mathematical precision.

every day -> Repeats every calendar day every mon, fri at 8pm -> Strict day-of-week & time constraints every 1st wed -> Dynamic positional week calculations every! 2 months -> Completion-based (moves forward from when you finish) after 10 days -> Exact delay interval after checkoff every 12 hours starting at 9pm -> Sub-daily bounded recurrences

Boolean Filter Query Engine

Slice and dice your tasks with a custom compiler that evaluates dates, projects, labels, and priorities in sub-millisecond execution.

(today | overdue) & #Research -> High-priority focus for ongoing papers p1 & overdue, p4 & today -> Critical items + low-stress today tasks ##Engineering & %bologna -> Nested project trees with label filters due before: next monday & !assigned -> Tasks requiring personal triage this week

Field-Level Last-Write-Wins (LWW)

If your phone changes a due date while your laptop renames the title offline, both edits merge flawlessly without losing changes or requiring complex conflict resolution dialogues.

• SQLite Triggers log changed fields to a local dirty queue • Incremental sync pushes only deltas upon reconnection • Android WorkManager keeps background reminders refreshed every ~15 mins • Windows Toast notifications fire even when the app is completely closed
