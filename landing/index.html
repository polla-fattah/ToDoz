THE MANIFESTO

The ToDoz Philosophy

Modern productivity software has lost its way into subscription paywalls, tracking telemetry, and sluggish cloud bloat. ToDoz is a reference implementation of Sovereign Personal Software, built around four foundational principles with AI used to lower the cost of building it, not to add weight to the product.

"Software should serve people without claiming permanent custody over their information, choices, or work."
Read the full manifesto →
PRINCIPLE I
Sovereignty
Authority belongs to the user. Tasks live in a local SQLite database you control, with self-hosted sync, revocable device tokens, and freedom from mandatory cloud accounts.
PRINCIPLE II
Longevity
Information outlives software. Documented SQLite schemas, portable exports (JSON, CSV, ICS), and verified backups keep your commitments readable for decades.
PRINCIPLE III
Locality
Independence is designed in. Creating, completing, searching, and filtering tasks work completely offline; synchronization is an optional transport, never a prerequisite.
PRINCIPLE IV
Transparency & Control
Consequential behavior remains governable. Recurrence rules and Boolean filters are deterministic and testable, every change is logged with Undo, and sync rules are disclosed honestly.
SUPPORTING QUALITY · T6
Interoperability & Maintainability
Focused task execution that connects to Markdown vaults, journals, and calendars via documented REST APIs, signed webhooks, ICS feeds, and CSV/JSON interchange.
SUPPORTING QUALITY · T7
Human Experience & Speed
Immediate sub-100ms feedback on local SQLite operations, keyboard-first Quick Add, and respectful notifications without dark patterns or cloud spinners.

ENGINEERING FOUNDATIONS

Built with Craftsmanship

Modeled on Todoist’s peak workflow speed and enhanced with native offline database storage and self-hosted control.

100% Offline SQLite Store
Runs on Drift SQLite. Complete tasks, reorder boards, and filter views instantaneously on planes, trains, or offline server rooms.
Mathematical Recurrence
Supports every! 2 months (completion-based), every 1st wed, every last workday, and explicit refusal of ambiguous grammar.
Google Calendar Time-Grid
Native 15-minute slot time-grid in Day, 3-Day, Week, and Month layouts. Drag to reschedule or adjust durations with live Google Calendar sync.
Boolean Filter Engine
Full query grammar: (today | overdue) & #Research, p1 & overdue, p4 & today, and due before: next monday.
Zero-Password Pairing
Join devices via short-lived cryptographic pairing codes. No plaintext passwords or email verification traps required.
Self-Hosted Sync Server
Lightweight Docker service using field-level Last-Write-Wins (LWW) conflict resolution and Tailscale mesh support.
VIEW DIMENSIONS

One Task Model. Three Perspectives.

Switch between chronological time blocks, visual kanban pipelines, and hierarchical project trees.

Drawn natively with a "now" marker line, side-by-side overlapping event handling, and 15-minute snap handles:

• Day View - Micro-schedule today's hours with exact 15m time blocks • 3-Day View - Multi-day tactical sprint planning • Week View - Broad 7-day operational horizon • Month View - High-level bird's-eye view with drag-and-drop reschedule

Mathematical natural language recurrence parsed offline with deterministic precision:

every day -> Repeats every calendar day every mon, fri at 8pm -> Strict day-of-week & time constraints every 1st wed -> Dynamic positional week calculations every! 2 months -> Completion-based (schedules from when you finish) after 10 days -> Exact delay interval after checkoff

Boolean query language evaluated across priorities, dates, labels, and project hierarchies:

(today | overdue) & #Research -> High-priority focus for active research papers p1 & overdue, p4 & today -> Critical items + low-stress items ##Engineering & %bologna -> Sub-projects matching specific tags due before: next monday & !assigned -> Tasks requiring personal triage this week

Field-level Last-Write-Wins (LWW) ensures offline multi-device edits merge cleanly:

• SQLite Triggers record modified fields to a local dirty log • Incremental sync transfers only delta changes upon network reconnection • Android WorkManager keeps background reminders refreshed every ~15 mins • Windows Toast notifications fire even when the application is completely closed
